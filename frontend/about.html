About the Project

Abstract, introduction, objectives, scope and the research behind AgriCare AI.

Abstract

A Free, Fast Crop Doctor

AgriCare AI is a web application that detects crop diseases instantly from leaf photographs. A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on the PlantVillage dataset of 54,000+ labelled images classifies leaves into 38 classes across 14 crop species.

The farmer uploads a photo, and the model returns the disease name, a confidence score and recommended treatment within two seconds — accessible from any smartphone or PC with almost no training. Model reports a target accuracy above 95%.

Why It Matters

The Problem We Solve

❌ Existing System

  • Manual visual inspection — late and unreliable
  • Laboratory PCR / culture tests — slow, costly, scarce
  • Dependence on scarce plant pathologists
  • Bulk, unsafe pesticide application
  • No digital record of farm health
  • Research demos never become usable products

✅ Proposed System

  • Instant AI diagnosis from a phone photo
  • Lightweight CNN (MobileNetV2 / ResNet50)
  • Free, web-based, anytime-anywhere access
  • Condition-specific organic treatment advice
  • Stored diagnosis history per farm
  • Scalable, low-cost, farmer-friendly

Research Review

Literature Survey

2016

Mohanty, Hughes & Salathé

Trained AlexNet & GoogLeNet on PlantVillage — up to 99.35% accuracy on held-out images.

2017

Sladojevic et al.

Fine-tuned deep network in Caffe: 96.3% accuracy across 13 crop disease classes.

2019

Barbedo (Review)

CNNs are state of the art, but sensitive to real-world background, lighting and growth stage.

2021

Too et al.

Transfer learning (VGG16, Inception, ResNet, DenseNet) maximises accuracy with less data.

2022

Agarwal et al.

MobileNetV2 tomato-leaf CNN at 98.7% — lightweight, ideal for low-cost smartphones.

2024

Recent Advances

Ensembles, vision transformers and Grad-CAM add robustness and explainability.

🔎 Gap Identified

Existing studies focus on standalone model accuracy. Few deliver an end-to-end, free, web-based product with treatment advice and diagnosis history in local languages — exactly what AgriCare AI provides.
