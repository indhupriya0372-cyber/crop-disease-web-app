    Photo Tips

    Take a Better Photo, Get a Better Result

    📷

    Good Light

    Shoot in daylight, not harsh sun — avoid shadows and glare on the leaf.

    🎯

    One Leaf, Full Frame

    Crop tightly around a single leaf so the affected area fills the frame.

    🖐️

    Stable Hand

    Hold the phone steady or place it on a book to avoid motion blur.

    🌞

    Both Sides

    If unsure, run the top side first — many moulds show on the underside too.
